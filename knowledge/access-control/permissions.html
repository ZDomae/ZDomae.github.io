Account Admin

The Account Admin role is used for internal engineers to set up the product for new customers.

SectionDescription
Admin PortalCreate and manage global settings and classes.
ProductManage and edit all sections in the product.
Users & AccountsCreate and manages users and groups.
AssessmentsCreate and manage operational acceptance testing assessment types.
ReportsImport and export Power BI reports and templates.
DashboardsImport and export dashboards.

Account Compliance Officer

The Account Compliance Officer is responsible for managing the Compliance part of the product.

SectionDescription
Admin PortalManage Compliance classes.
ProductRestricted.
Users & AccountsCreate and manages users and groups for the Compliance product.
AssessmentsManage existing Compliance assessment types.
ReportsImport and export Power BI reports and templates.
DashboardsImport and export dashboards.
